About

Brew It is a coffee journal for people who like to experiment. Save your recipes, record every brew, and keep track of what you change along the way.

Every recipe has versions. When you try something new, like a finer grind or a different dose, save it as a new version, and your old ones stay in the history. Older versions highlight what's different from what you brew now, so you can see what changed.

Your recipes are saved only in this browser, on this device. Clearing your browser data will delete them. When you use Ask AI, that one recipe and your question are sent to an AI (Anthropic's Claude) to get an answer. Brew It doesn't save them.

Ask AI is here: ask a question about any recipe, like how to make it less bitter or a pour plan, and get suggestions to try as a new version. AI answers can be wrong. Coming soon: creating new recipes with AI.

Built by Roey Madjar, a junior frontend developer, as a portfolio project. Made with React and the Claude API.
